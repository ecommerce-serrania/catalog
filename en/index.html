Why companies choose us

Your team's clothing speaks before they do

A badly cut garment ends up stuck in the closet and only comes out on photo day. A well-made one gets worn on weekends, outside the office, by choice. That difference is not a manufacturing detail: it is the difference between handing out uniforms and building a brand people want to wear.

That is why we treat every style as a fashion piece that happens to carry a logo, and not the other way around. Companies like the ones above do not come to us because we make jackets. They come because they understand that what their team wears is a statement about who they are.

01

In-house design and patternmaking

Every style is developed in our own factory, with separate patterns for men and women and real size charts. We do not resell someone else's catalog.

02

Production under our own roof

We cut, sew and inspect in Cajica, Colombia. No middlemen: quality control is ours and any adjustment is handled immediately.

03

Branding done right

We choose the technique your logo deserves, not the cheapest to apply. Embroidery, laser etching or sublimation completely change how a brand reads on fabric.

Before you order

Frequently asked questions

Is this the same as a uniform program?

No. Uniforms get handed out and end up in a drawer; we design pieces the team actually wants to wear, outside work too. Same function, very different result.

What is the minimum order?

50 units per style for most items. The satin scarf starts at 200 units. You can mix sizes and colors within the same order.

Can I order men's and women's sizes in the same order?

Yes. Several styles have separate patterns by gender, and each product page shows both size charts.

How do we decide sizes for the team?

Each product page includes the size guide with a reference drawing. You measure a garment you already own, laid flat. Measurements may vary by up to 0.8 in (2 cm).

Is every color always available?

The colors shown on each page depend on fabric availability in stock. We confirm inventory when quoting, before the order is closed.

Do you make custom designs that are not in the catalog?

Yes. We work from your own design or adapt an existing style. Write to us with the idea or a reference photo.

Tell us what your team needs

Write to us with the item and the quantity. We reply with pricing, lead times and a digital mockup of your brand on the garment.
